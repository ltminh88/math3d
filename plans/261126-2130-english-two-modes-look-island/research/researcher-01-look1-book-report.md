# Researcher 01 — LOOK 1 textbook survey (for our own exercises)

Source: a locally held copy of a commercial Level-1 kids' coursebook (image scans, not in the repo).
Detailed per-unit notes live in a local-only file and are never committed. Everything here is our own
wording: topic names, single words and generic sentence templates — no book text.

## Shape of the book

- Starter (4 short lessons) + **12 units**, 8 pages each, plus a review/extra block after every 2 units.
- Every unit follows the same 7-lesson template: Words (8–10 picture words) → Grammar A (statement
  pattern) → Reading (3–5 extra words) → Grammar B (question / negative form) → Song → Phonics → Video
  (ask & answer).
- Extras: 3 classroom-language pages, 3 game boards, 3 short reading extras (day/night, a frog tale,
  colour mixing), 6 reviews, bonus pages.
- **Spelling: American** (color, favorite, mom, eraser, pants, candy, soccer, store, vacation, center).
- Named illustrated kids recur (only a handful, e.g. Xi, Kaitlyn, Shiven, Olga, Marcel) plus a teacher in
  the classroom-language pages. No mascot.

## Sentence-pattern templates (our wording)

Starter: `Hello! My name's ___.` · `I'm ___ (age).` · `I'm fine, thanks.`
1. `What's this? — It's a/an ___.` · `Is it a ___? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.`
2. `This is my ___.` · `Is this your ___?`
3. `He's / She's a ___.` · `Is he/she a ___? — Yes, he is. / No, she isn't.`
4. `I have a ___.` · `I don't have a ___.` · `His / Her name is ___.`
5. `This is my ___. / These are my ___s.` · `He has ___ hair.` · `She doesn't have ___ eyes.`
6. `Where's the ___? — It's in the ___.` · `The ___ is on / under / next to the ___.`
7. `There's a ___ in the ___.` · `There are ___s.`
8. `A ___ can ___.` · `A ___ can't ___.` · `Can a ___ ___? — Yes, it can. / No, it can't.`

10. `I like ___.` · `I don't like ___.` · `Do you like ___? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.`
11. `There isn't a ___.` · `There aren't ___s.` · `Is there / Are there ___?`
12. `Let's ___! — Great idea!` · `We're in/at/on the ___.` · `We aren't at ___.`

## Suggested stage mapping (English island, LOOK mode)

- **13 stages**: Stage 0 = Starter (numbers, colours, greetings); Stages 1–12 = one per unit.
- ~8 rounds per stage, e.g.: 2× picture→word, 1× word→picture (listen), 1× pattern pick
  (yes/no or he/she, this/these, can/can't, like/don't like…), 1× build-the-sentence (drag 3–4 tiles),
  1× phonics (first letter for 1–6, missing vowel in CVC for 7–12), 1× reading-extra word, 1× mixed review.
- Optional checkpoint stage after every 2 units (mirrors the book's reviews) = 6 boss/review stages.
- Total ≈ 13 × 8 ≈ 104 rounds (+48 if review stages added).

## How it differs from the CEC book

| | CEC (current) | LOOK 1 |
|-|---------------|--------|
| Core | Phonics A–Z, one letter per unit | Topic vocabulary + grammar per unit |
| Sentences | Set phrases only, no grammar | 2 explicit patterns per unit (questions, negatives, plurals, can, like, there is/are, prepositions) |
| Phonics | Letter sounds, tracing, writing | A–Z initial sounds compressed into units 1–6, then short-vowel CVC (a e i o u) |
| Reading | None beyond words | Short photo texts, reading extras |
| Order | Alphabetical | Thematic (school → self → home → town → nature → free time) |
| Spelling | British (colour…) | American (color…) |

Implication: LOOK needs a sentence-level exercise type (tile ordering / fill-the-gap with 2–3 choices),
which CEC never needed; letter work can reuse CEC exercise types.

## Unresolved questions

1. Spelling: show American spellings as the book does, or normalise to the British used for CEC? (Mixed UI is confusing.)
2. Include the Reading-lesson extra words (~45) or stick to core Words-lesson vocab only?
3. Unit 12 vocab is verb phrases — picture cards need action images; is our art pipeline ready for that?
4. Do we add the 6 review/checkpoint stages, or keep 13 stages flat?
5. Can a grade-1 child type/drag full sentences, or should LOOK rounds stay at 2–3-choice picks with audio?
6. Should the phonics strand of units 1–6 be skipped when the child already finished CEC (same A–Z content)?
