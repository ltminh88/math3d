## B1 Ôn tập các số đến 1000 (pp 6-8)
- Range: 3-digit numbers 100-999, boundaries 1000, 999, 100; 2-digit numbers appear in "liền trước/liền sau".
- Forms:
  1. Read/write number from base-10 blocks (hundreds flats / tens rods / unit cubes) in table Trăm|Chục|Đơn vị|Viết số|Đọc số (fill missing cells; read in words).
  2. Matching (nối) description "số gồm X trăm Y chục Z đơn vị" -> number (plain case; case with 0 chục; case with 0 đơn vị; all-9s boundary case).
  3. Fill table: number -> digits trăm/chục/đơn vị (and reverse, including numbers with zero tens and/or zero ones digits).
  4. Expand number into sum of hundreds + tens + ones (viết thành tổng trăm, chục, đơn vị).
  5. Số liền trước / số liền sau (3-digit and 2-digit numbers, incl. crossing a tens/hundreds boundary and the 999/1000 edge).
  6. Three consecutive numbers on a tia số (mini number line around 10..20) and "tìm số ở ô ? để được ba số liên tiếp" (ascending and descending order, gap at end or middle).
  7. So sánh >, <, = with: plain 3-digit (same digits rearranged), near numbers across a hundred boundary, vs expression (number vs sum of hundreds+tens+ones; number vs 2-term +/-; expression vs expression).
  8. Điền số trên tia/dãy số đếm: sequence step 1 with several gaps, descending sequence starting at 1000.
  9. Sắp xếp 4 numbers (same three digits permuted) tăng dần / giảm dần.
  10. Word problem 1-step "so sánh" -> weights of 3 animals given as numbers near 100 kg + descriptions (nặng nhất / nhẹ nhất / vừa) -> read answer. Theme: farm animals.
- Visual models: Hundreds/tens/units block images + place-value table; số tia (number line with highlighted trio); ladder/chain of circles (sequence).
- Carry/borrow: none.
- Skills (tier):
  - num1000-read-write (T1: 3-digit no zero; T2: with zero digit; T3: word <-> digits)
  - num1000-place-value (T1: match hundreds-tens-ones; T2: zero digits; T3: expanded sum)
  - num1000-neighbors (T1: liền trước/sau within tens; T2: cross hundred boundary 299/300, 999/1000; T3: three consecutive)
  - num1000-compare (T1: different hundreds; T2: same hundreds; T3: vs expression a+b+c)
  - num1000-sequence-skip (T1: step 1 up; T2: step 1 down + crossing 1000; T3: mixed gaps)
  - num1000-order (T1: 3 numbers; T2: 4 numbers same digits, asc/desc)
  - num1000-wp-compare (T2)

## B2 Ôn tập phép cộng, phép trừ trong phạm vi 1000 (pp 9-10)
- Range: sum/diff within 1000 (inclusive 1000 as total). Tính nhẩm uses round tens/hundreds.
- Forms:
  1. Tính nhẩm ~12 items: round tens +/- (sum ≤ 100), round hundreds +/- (incl. total = 1000 and 1000 − hundreds).
  2. Đặt tính rồi tính: 2-digit +/- with carry/borrow (incl. sums reaching exactly 100 and 100 − 2-digit), 3-digit + 2-digit, 3-digit − 3-digit with borrow.
  3. Fill table: số hạng + số hạng -> tổng (2-digit and 3-digit + 2-digit); số bị trừ - số trừ -> hiệu (incl. 1000 − hundreds, 100 − 2-digit, 3-digit − 3-digit).
  4. Chuỗi phép tính (flow chain): start number, + hundreds-ish, then − hundreds-ish (two operations in sequence).
  5. So sánh kết quả: which expressions give result greater than a threshold / find pairs with equal results (ghép lọ with 5-6 mixed +/- expressions); a "find equal pair" type.
  6. Word problem 1-step add or subtract: weights of two animals in hundreds of kg (tổng and hơn kém).
  7. Word problem 2-step: "nhóm A nhiều hơn nhóm B n học sinh" -> step1 find the other group, step2 total. Theme: school classes/grades.
- Visual: vertical-form column calc; table; chain-flow diagram.
- Carry/borrow: yes in 2-digit & 3-digit; has pattern 2-digit + 2-digit = 100 (carry to hundreds), 100 − 2-digit borrow across tens and hundreds (borrow chain through 0), 3-digit − 3-digit double borrow.
- Skills:
  - addsub1000-mental-round (T1 tens, T2 hundreds & to 1000)
  - addsub1000-vertical (T1: no carry 3-digit; T2: carry once; T3: carry twice / borrow across zero, e.g. 100 − 2-digit)
  - addsub1000-table (T2), addsub1000-chain (T2: 2 ops), addsub1000-compare-results (T3)
  - addsub1000-wp1 (T1 tổng / hơn kém; T2 nhiều hơn / ít hơn), addsub1000-wp2 (T3: find-one-then-total)

## B14 Một phần mấy (pp 42-45)  [Grade-3 new concept: unit fractions 1/2..1/9 only]
- Range: denominators 2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9. Always numerator 1 (unit fractions). Counting sets up to 12 objects.
- Forms:
  1. Introduction: round shape cut into 2 or 4 equal parts, color 1 part -> "một phần hai/tư" written 1/2, 1/4.
  2. Đ/S: "Đã tô màu 1/n hình chữ nhật/hình tròn" with bars divided into n columns (n=2..6) or circles into n sectors (n=4..9); includes traps (parts of unequal NUMBER vs shaded part not 1, mismatch denominator).
  3. Nối cách đọc ("một phần năm/tư/hai/ba") with fraction written on shape slices.
  4. Which picture shaded 1/n: square cut along diagonals (triangles), small square grids, vertical strips -> choose A/B/C/D (equal-part check: all parts must be equal; distractor shapes).
  5. Fraction of a SET: "khoanh vào 1/n số đồ vật" (set size divisible by n, set ≤ ~10 items).
  6. Fill: "chia N đồ vật thành n phần bằng nhau, 1/n số đồ vật là ? " (N ≤ 12).
- Visual models: circle sectors, rectangle bars, square grids, slice shapes, groups of objects — the game core needs SVG shape renderer with n equal parts + k shaded.
- Rules: only equal parts valid; fraction-of-set results are whole numbers (set size divisible by n).
- Skills:
  - fraction-unit-shaded (T1: n=2,3,4 circle/bar; T2: n=5-9; T3: grid/odd shapes + unequal-parts trap)
  - fraction-unit-read (T1: word<->symbol n<=4; T2 n<=9)
  - fraction-unit-identify-pick (T2: which of 4 pictures shows 1/n)
  - fraction-unit-of-set-circle (T1: n=2 with <=8 items; T2: n=3-5; T3 up to 9)
  - fraction-unit-of-set-number (T2: "1/n of N" = N/n; N ≤ ~12 in book; extend T3 to N<=30 via chia)
  - fraction-wp (T3: B41 has a fraction-of-a-set word item; see HK1)

## B23 Nhân số có hai chữ số với số có một chữ số (pp 67-69)  [in textbook phạm vi 100 — product < 100]
- Range: 2-digit x 1-digit; all products ≤ 99 in lesson (multiplicand roughly ≤ 33 for x3, ≤ 19 for x5).
- Forms:
  1. Introduction: 2-digit x 1-digit as repeated addition (equal groups), then vertical.
  2. Tính (đặt tính theo cột dọc given): without carry (ones digit x multiplier < 10) and with carry (one carry from ones into tens).
  3. Tính nhẩm theo mẫu: round tens x 1-digit (a chục x b = ab chục).
  4. Word problem 1-step "mỗi ... có, ... có tất cả" (n containers x 2-digit items each).
  5. Match results to letters (decode puzzle) (game: puzzle code).
  6. Điền chữ số / swap two digit-cards to make a correct product — missing-digit puzzle; T3.
- Carry rules: carry only from ones to tens; result stays 2-digit (≤99); worked example pattern "ones x digit = 1x, viết x nhớ 1" then "tens x digit, thêm 1".
- Visual: repeated-groups boxes, vertical form.
- Skills:
  - mul-2d-1d-nocarry (T1: ones x digit <10),
  - mul-2d-1d-carry (T2: product ≤99, carry 1-2), mul-2d-1d-mental-tens (T1 round tens x digit),
  - mul-2d-1d-wp1 (T2), mul-2d-1d-missing-digit (T3)

## B26 Chia số có hai chữ số cho số có một chữ số (pp 75-78)
- Range: dividend 2-digit (~33..98), divisors 2..7. Quotient 2-digit or 1-digit.
- Forms:
  1. Introduction: chia đều a 2-digit quantity into 2 containers (long division, no remainder, tens divisible).
  2. Tính (chia hết): tens digit divisible by divisor; quotient 2-digit.
  3. Tính nhẩm theo mẫu: round tens : 1-digit (a chục : b = c chục).
  4. Tìm thừa số chưa biết: a x ? = P, ? x a = P (bài toán ngược: dùng phép chia).
  5. Tens digit not divisible → hạ chữ số (exact case); and case with remainder (có dư).
  6. Điền thương và số dư (fill long-division skeleton), 4 items with remainders.
  7. Word problem: 1-step chia đều; plus "n người / bàn" seating -> số bàn cần (quotient with dư → round up, "cần ít nhất").
  8. Tìm các phép chia có số dư là k (from a list of ~5 divisions: choose those with remainder k — ensure dư < số chia).
  9. Tìm số bị chia: ? : n = q (inverse).
  10. Weights of equal groups: n identical animals/objects weigh W kg total → each? (exact division).
- Remainder rules: remainder < divisor (dư luôn nhỏ hơn số chia); quotient/dư written "(dư r)"; remainder 0 = chia hết; answers "cần ít nhất" round UP quotient when dư > 0.
- Visual: long-division skeleton (vertical bracket), chia đều into containers.
- Skills:
  - div-2d-1d-exact (T1: tens divisible; T2: need hạ; T3 divisor up to 9)
  - div-2d-1d-remainder (T1: dư with small divisor 2-3; T2: divisors 4-7; T3: pick all with dư = k, remainder<divisor trap)
  - div-mental-tens (T1: round tens : digit)
  - div-find-missing-factor (T2: a x ? = P) , div-find-dividend (T2/T3: ? : n = q)
  - div-2d-1d-wp (T1 chia đều; T2 chia có dư + "bao nhiêu hộp cần"; T3 round-up)

## B36 Nhân số có ba chữ số với số có một chữ số (pp 97-98)
- Range: 3-digit x 1-digit, product always ≤ 999 in lesson.
- Forms: Tính (vertical given); Đặt tính rồi tính (items horizontal); Tính nhẩm round hundreds x digit (a trăm x b = ab trăm); fill table (thừa số x thừa số -> tích); word problem 1-step "gấp n lần"; 1-step with equal weights (balance-scale context); 2-step (n containers x volume, then minus amount used → remaining).
- Carry rules: ones carry (ones x digit ≥ 10, carry 1-3), tens carry; no carry into hundreds beyond 999; zero digits inside the multiplicand (0 tens or 0 ones) = tricky.
- Visual: vertical form; (balance scale for weights; cubes stacked).
- Skills:
  - mul-3d-1d-nocarry (T1: every digit x multiplier < 10), mul-3d-1d-carry (T2: one/two carries; T3: with 0 digit in multiplicand), mul-hundreds-mental (T1 round hundreds x digit), mul-3d-1d-table (T2), mul-3d-1d-wp1 ("gấp N lần", T2), mul-3d-1d-wp2 (T3: multiply then subtract)

## B37 Chia số có ba chữ số cho số có một chữ số (pp 99-103)
- Range: dividend 3-digit (102..999), divisors 2..9.
- Forms:
  1. Intro (4 worked cases): first digit ≥ divisor → quotient 3-digit; first digit < divisor → lấy 2 chữ số (quotient 2-digit, with dư); zero in quotient (exact); zero in quotient with remainder.
  2. Tính: ~12 items mixing chia hết and có dư, divisors 3-9.
  3. Đ/S check on worked solutions — tìm lỗi sai in quotient/remainder (dropped zero in quotient is the typical error; also dư ≥ số chia; missing quotient digit).
  4. Đặt tính rồi tính theo mẫu: 4 items incl. zero-in-quotient cases.
  5. Tính nhẩm round hundreds : 1-digit (a trăm : b = c trăm).
  6. Word problem 1-step chia: n items packed k per box → number of boxes; equal-share weight.
  7. "Giảm n lần": a quantity (m, phút, ml, g) giảm n lần = quantity : n (new concept: giảm đi một số lần = chia).
  8. Chọn đáp án multi-step puzzle (score from hits under point rules); 2-step counting puzzle with two kinds of animals (algebra-ish, optional).
- Remainder rules: dư < số chia; remainder 0 vs non-zero; quotient may contain 0 digit (in middle or at end).
- Visual: long-division bracket; 'giảm n lần' bar diagram; stacks of items → boxes.
- Skills:
  - div-3d-1d-exact (T1: first digit divisible; T2: need 2-digit start; T3: zero in quotient)
  - div-3d-1d-remainder (T2: dư simple; T3: zero in quotient + dư)
  - div-hundreds-mental (T1 round hundreds : digit)
  - div-3d-1d-find-error (T3: Đ/S pick wrong long-division record)
  - div-reduce-times (T2: "giảm n lần")
  - div-3d-1d-wp1 (T2: chia đều; T3: remainder → thêm hộp "cần ít nhất")

## B41 Ôn tập nhân chia 100/1000 (pp 113-115)
- Forms: tính nhẩm mix (round tens x/: digit; round hundreds x/: digit); đặt tính (2-digit x/: 1-digit; 3-digit x/: 1-digit); Đ/S correct column calculation (find-error: carry forgotten, invalid dư ≥ divisor, dropped 0 in quotient); 1-step wp (pack into boxes; n vehicles x k riders; liquid into cans → round up); điền chữ số thích hợp (missing digits in a 3-digit x 1-digit or 2-digit x 1-digit product); multi-choice (product, quotient, remainder, missing factor); "gấp n lần" 2-step (find the larger amount, then total); fraction of a set (1/n of a small set); Đố em: place given digits into ?? x ? to hit a target product.
- Skills reuse: mul-/div- skills above + fraction-unit-of-set-number + muldiv-find-error (T3) + muldiv-missing-digit (T3).

## B42 Ôn tập biểu thức số (pp 116-117)
- Forms: tính giá trị biểu thức: 2 operations same level no brackets (+/− left to right, x/: left to right) and mixed (x or : with + or −) — nhân chia trước, cộng trừ sau; with brackets (bracket inside +, −, :, x); match items to expression values (bracket and no-bracket mix); 3 operations same level (3-term sum, 3-factor product); wp 2-step (n bags x weight + one other bag; total items : k : m → containers); "đố em" chọn dấu +,−,×,: để biểu thức lớn nhất/bé nhất or equals a target.
- Order of operations is the key rule (nhân chia trước, brackets first).
- Skills: expr-two-ops (T1: same level left to right; T2: mixed × then +), expr-brackets (T2/T3), expr-pick-value (T2 match), expr-wp2 (T3), expr-choose-operator (T3).
